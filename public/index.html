How to use Interview Coach

  1. Add a job. Paste or upload your résumé and the posting. Screenshots work.
  2. Confirm your facts. Each shows the line it came from. Only confirmed facts get used.
  3. See how you match. Your job hub lines up their asks with your experience. No fake scores.
  4. Practice the interview. Recruiter, hiring manager, panel or senior exec. Type it or talk it.
  5. Read the debrief, then drill. Feedback on that run, then practice cards for your details.
  6. Walk in ready. Build a Prep Pack, draft a résumé or cover letter, log the real interview.

    Help: stuck or confused?
    

      
What are "facts"?
Short, true statements pulled from what you gave us, each with the line it came from. Only the ones you confirm are used in your practice and writing.

      
It's taking a long time.
The AI step can take up to 30 seconds. Keep the tab open. If it stops, tap Try again or refresh. Your work is saved.

      
I got an error.
Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, take a screenshot and send it to whoever runs this site.

      
The microphone won't work.
Your browser asks for permission the first time. Tap Allow. If you said no, tap the lock icon by the address bar and allow the microphone. Typing your answers works too.

      
Who can see my stuff?
Guest work is kept in this browser tab and clears when you tap End session. Signed-in work is saved to your account, and the site operator can read its database. AI steps send the text or files needed for that step to the configured AI provider. Voice calls also go to Retell. You can delete your account any time.

      
Why does it keep asking me to confirm things?
You check the details pulled from your sources before they're used. If a fact is wrong, fix it. If it's right, confirm it.

      
What if my sources disagree?
My Facts shows possible conflicts with both source quotes. You can also select two details to flag a conflict. Fix a detail, set it aside, or explain when each applies and resolve the review. Unresolved pairs stay out of new practice and writing evidence. Your original sources and review history stay available.

      
I forgot my password.
There's no reset email yet. Ask whoever runs this site to reset it for you.
